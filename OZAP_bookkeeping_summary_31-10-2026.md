In 2026 OZAP also sent her money that was not a payback:

- €10,000 on 16 June 2026
- €500 on 16 July 2026
- One payback of €1,199.85 was sent twice by mistake (12 September 2025)

This is OZAP money that Zsofia has, so she owes it back. That makes it a loan from OZAP to her. It is on the balance sheet as something OZAP will get back (account 110500).

She pays back €500 and €199.85 in November. After that the loan is **€11,000**. She must pay it back by **16 June 2027**. There is no interest. The agreement is attached in Odoo.

The loan stays under £10,000, so no shareholder approval or extra tax is needed.
