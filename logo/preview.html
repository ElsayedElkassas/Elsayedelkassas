Socius Finance

Two monogram directions where the S and F share strokes. Socius is Latin for partner, so in both marks one letter cannot stand without the other.

A · Interlock

Recommended

The F's top bar and stem curve into the top half of an S. The gold stroke finishes the S. Read the dark shape alone and you get F. Read the full form and you get S. Compact and square, so it works as an app icon and favicon.

Wider, more of a wordmark feel

One continuous top line runs from the S into the F. The S hands over to the F at the exact point where the stem drops. Reads as "SF" straight away. Better for letterheads and signage than for small icons.
